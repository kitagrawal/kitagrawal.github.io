Contact & Concerns

Effective date: 12 November 2026

For questions about The Applied AI Letter, payments, privacy or this website, email support@appliedailetter.com. We aim to reply within 5 business days.

Subscription and billing

To cancel your plan, use the Cancel link at the bottom of any letter, or email support@appliedailetter.com. Cancelling ends your plan at the end of the period you have paid for, and letters continue until then. For payment problems, unexpected charges or refund requests, email support@appliedailetter.com from the address you subscribed with, or include it, with a short description. Never send card numbers, passwords or verification codes.

Privacy requests

To access, correct, delete or export your personal information, email support@appliedailetter.com with the subject "Privacy Request". We may confirm that you own the email address before disclosing or changing anything, and we reply within 30 days or any shorter period the law requires.

Editorial corrections and other concerns

To report a factual error, an intellectual property concern, an accessibility problem or a website fault, email support@appliedailetter.com with a link to the page or issue and the details.
