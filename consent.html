Email & Cookie Consent

Effective date: 12 November 2026

This page explains how you choose to receive emails from The Applied AI Letter and how the Site handles cookies. It supplements our Privacy Policy.

1. Paid newsletter delivery

When you subscribe, we use your email address to deliver the paid Newsletter and essential messages about your subscription: a welcome email, and messages about billing, renewals, cancellation and access. Stripe also emails you receipts and failed-payment notices. These messages are part of the service you requested.

2. Optional marketing

We do not send promotional emails unrelated to your subscription, and we do not share your email address with anyone for marketing. If that changes, we will ask for your consent first, with an opt-in that is not preselected, and record your choice.

3. Cancelling

Every letter has a Cancel link at the bottom; you can also email support@appliedailetter.com. Cancelling ends your plan at the end of the period you have paid for: letters continue until then and you are not charged again. See Cancellation in the Terms.

4. Cookies and tracking choices

The Site uses no analytics, advertising or other non-essential cookies, and it sets no cookies of its own, so there is nothing to accept or reject. It stores one preference, your light or dark theme, in your browser's local storage; you can clear it in your browser settings at any time. Google Fonts receives your IP address when the Site's fonts load. Stripe's checkout and the Cal.com booking window set cookies they need to work and to prevent fraud, under their own policies. If we ever add non-essential tracking, we will ask for your consent before it runs and let you change your choice later.

5. Withdrawal and records

You can withdraw any consent at any time without affecting processing already lawfully carried out. We keep a record of cancelled plans so that we do not email you again by mistake. Contact support@appliedailetter.com.
